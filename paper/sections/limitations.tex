\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limits}

This section states what the study does not cover and what would extend it.

\textbf{Data.} Labels come from a public set; one of 80 checked photos looked like another class, and no label was
changed. Backgrounds are plain, so the results say nothing about cluttered field scenes. There are no severity labels, only
four classes are shared with Paddy Doctor, bacterial leaf blight is absent, and all photos come from one region and three
cameras.

\textbf{Method.} The encoder is frozen and never adapted to rice. Each photo needs about 79 encoder passes, which
costs time (Section~\ref{sec:results}) and, with the cached descriptions of eight versions per photo, disk space. Zero-shot
scores depend on the zoom, and the leaf-scale rule rests on 29 measurements and is borderline.

\textbf{Evidence.} Each round has one training seed, so the SD measures variation over folds. A photo that is a validation
photo in one round is a test photo in another, so tuning is not fully independent of the pooled test photos. The
session-blocked split and several paired tests were declared after related means were known, and tuning budgets differ
across method families. Timing comes from one machine. The judgements listed in Section~\ref{sec:setup} come from model
raters of one family.

\textbf{Future work.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Check attention against lesion outlines (RiceSeg-5932 \cite{riceseg2025}) and run a rating study of the heat
maps with plant-disease experts.
\item Handle the other nine Paddy Doctor classes as unseen conditions (open-set), and add bacterial leaf blight.
\item Fine-tune the encoder lightly, and build and time an on-device version.
\item Use Dhan-Shomadhan \cite{hossain2021} as a further test set.
\end{itemize}
